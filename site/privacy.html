Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pasabuy Tracker (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use this website (fasthubeyy.site), the Pasabuy Tracker web app (app.fasthubeyy.site) and the Pasabuy Tracker Android app.

1. Information you enter in the app

The app lets you record trips, customers (names, contact details, addresses, emails), orders, item photos, payments, receipt screenshots and delivery photos. This information is stored only on your own device — in the app’s local database on Android, or in your browser’s storage for the web app. We do not run a server that receives or stores it, and we cannot see it.

You are responsible for the customer information you choose to record and should only collect what you need for your orders. You can delete records in the app, reset all data from Settings, or export and keep your own backup file.

2. Device permissions

The Android app asks for camera and photo access only when you choose to attach an item photo, payment receipt or delivery photo, or to save the donation QR code to your gallery. These files stay on your device.

3. This website and advertising

This website uses Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

4. Hosting and logs

The website and web app are hosted on Vercel, which may process standard technical information such as IP addresses and browser type in server logs to deliver and secure the service.

5. Links to other services

The app links to third-party services you choose to use, such as GCash/InstaPay QR payments and Ko-fi for optional donations. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

6. Children

Pasabuy Tracker is intended for adults running pasabuy businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Your rights

Because app data is stored on your device, you control it directly. For questions about this policy or about advertising data, contact us at serjomlio@gmail.com. If you are in the Philippines, you also have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
