Everything a pasabuy trip needs

From the first order request to the moment the bag changes hands.

Trips with a cutoff

Set your route, travel dates and order cutoff. The app counts down the days left to accept orders and closes the trip automatically after the cutoff.

Orders and items

Add several items per customer with photos, prices and quantities. Search and filter by paid, unpaid, bought, packed or delivered.

Fees done for you

Service fee percentage and per-kilo handling are calculated automatically, so you always see what the buyer pays and what you earn.

Luggage budget

Enter each order’s weight and watch your checked-baggage allowance fill up before you hit excess-baggage charges.

Payments and receipts

Log GCash, bank or cash payments with reference numbers and screenshots. Partial payments and balances are tracked per order.

Safe hand-over

Each order gets a 4-digit hand-over code and a checklist, plus a delivery photo, so there is never a dispute about who received what.
