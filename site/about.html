About Pasabuy Tracker

Pasabuy — “pasabay na bili” — is how many Filipinos working or travelling abroad help friends, family and customers back home get items that are expensive or hard to find in the Philippines. It is a real side business for thousands of OFWs and frequent travellers, but it is usually run on chat threads, notebooks and spreadsheets.

Pasabuy Tracker was built to replace that. It is a free app that keeps a whole pasabuy trip in one place: the route and order cutoff, every customer and their items, service and handling fees, luggage weight, payments with receipts, and the final hand-over.

What we believe

  • Simple first. No sign-up and no learning curve — open the app and create your first trip.
  • Your data is yours. Records are stored on your own device, and you can export a backup file at any time.
  • Fair for both sides. Clear fees, payment records and hand-over codes protect sellers and buyers alike.

Who makes it

Pasabuy Tracker is an independent project by a Filipino developer, made with feedback from pasabuy sellers. The app is free to use. This website is supported by advertising, and you can also support development directly from the More menu inside the app.
