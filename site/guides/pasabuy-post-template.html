Pasabuy post template for Facebook

Guide · 4 min read

A good pasabuy announcement answers buyers’ questions before they ask: where you are going, until when you accept orders, how much it costs and how to pay. Posts that leave these out get dozens of “hm?” comments and fewer real orders. Copy the template below and fill in your details.

The template

✈️ PASABUY: [City, Country] → [City in PH]
📅 Travel dates: [Departure date] – [Arrival date]
⏰ Order cutoff: [Date] (or until luggage is full)

🛍️ Can buy from: [stores / areas, e.g. Don Quijote, Uniqlo, drugstores]
🚫 Not accepting: [e.g. liquids over 100 ml, fragile items, frozen food]

💰 Fees
• Service fee: [15]% of item price
• Handling: ₱[150] per kg (weighed after packing)
• Price conversion: [your rule, e.g. today's GCash rate]

💳 Payment
• [50]% downpayment before I buy, balance before hand-over
• GCash / bank transfer — send screenshot + reference no.
• Downpayment is [non-refundable once item is bought]

📦 Hand-over
• Meet-up: [places] on [dates]
• Courier: [Lalamove / J&T], shipping fee paid by buyer

📩 How to order: comment "ORDER" and send me a message with
item name/link, size/colour, quantity and a photo.

Why each part matters

  • Route and dates tell buyers whether you can get what they want and when it will arrive.
  • A firm cutoff creates urgency and gives you time to shop and pack before your flight.
  • A “not accepting” list saves you from awkward refusals later.
  • Fees in plain numbers build trust. Buyers are much more comfortable when they can calculate the total themselves.
  • Payment rules in writing make downpayments and cancellations easy to enforce.
  • One clear way to order (for example, a direct message with a photo) keeps details out of messy comment threads.

After you post

As messages arrive, add each buyer’s order to your trip in Pasabuy Tracker. The app shows each buyer’s total including fees, keeps a running total of luggage weight, and lists who has paid their downpayment — so you can reply to “magkano po lahat?” in seconds.

Tip: pin a comment with your updated status (“12 kg left”, “cutoff tomorrow”). It answers the most common question without extra messages.
