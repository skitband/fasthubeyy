How to start a pasabuy side business

Guide · 7 min read

If you work abroad or travel often, friends have probably already asked you to “pasabuy” a few things. Turning those favours into a small, organised side business is mostly about setting clear rules early. This guide covers the first steps, from deciding what to offer to closing your first trip.

1. Decide what you will (and won’t) buy

Start with what you can get easily where you are and what is expensive or hard to find in the Philippines. Popular categories include skincare and cosmetics, vitamins, snacks and chocolates, kids’ items, sneakers and bags. Decide up front what you won’t accept: fragile items, very heavy items, liquids above airline limits, branded goods you can’t verify as authentic, and anything restricted by airlines or customs.

2. Write down your rules

Clear rules prevent most problems later. Before your first post, decide:

  • Fees – your service fee percentage and per-kilo handling charge (see How to price pasabuy orders).
  • Downpayment – how much buyers pay before you shop, and whether it is refundable.
  • Order cutoff – the last day you accept orders, usually a few days before your flight.
  • Substitutions – what happens if an item is out of stock or the price is different.
  • Hand-over – meet-up locations, courier options and who pays local shipping.

3. Plan your luggage budget

Check your actual baggage allowance, keep space for your own things, and work out how many kilos you can offer. If demand is high, compare the price of buying extra baggage in advance with what you’ll earn. Our luggage weight tips go through this in detail.

4. Announce your trip

Post in the places your buyers already are: your own Facebook profile, family and friends’ group chats, and pasabuy or OFW community groups that allow it. Include your route, travel dates, order cutoff, fees and payment methods. A ready-to-use format is in our pasabuy post template.

5. Keep every order organised

Once orders start coming in, chat threads quickly become unmanageable. For each buyer, record the items, quantities, prices, photos and how much they have paid. Pasabuy Tracker was made for exactly this: create a trip, add each customer’s order with its items, and the app calculates fees, buyer totals and your earnings while keeping track of luggage weight.

6. Collect downpayments before you shop

Only buy items once the downpayment is in. Log each payment with its reference number and screenshot so you can match it later — see Tracking downpayments and balances.

7. Shop, pack and weigh

Shop by store or area to save time, keep receipts, and pack each buyer’s items together. Weigh every order once packed and record the weight so handling fees and your luggage total are accurate.

8. Hand over and ask for feedback

Collect remaining balances, release items with a hand-over code and checklist, and take a delivery photo (see Safe and fair hand-overs). Afterwards, ask happy buyers if you can share their feedback — testimonials are the best advertising for your next trip.

Tip: start small. A first trip with five to ten trusted buyers teaches you more about pricing and packing than any guide, with much less risk.
